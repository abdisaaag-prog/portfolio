Automated Garden
System

Schulich School of Engineering  |  University of Calgary

Sensors Pump System Automation Sustainability Project Management

Objective

  • Design a system that autonomously manages soil moisture and grow lighting
  • Meet overflow prevention requirements while minimizing total water consumption

What I Did

  • Integrated moisture sensors, automated water pump, reservoir, and grow lighting
  • Designed mechanical enclosure, wiring layout, and sensor-driven control logic
  • Engineered a closed-loop water economy — perforated drainage holes feed a recirculating reservoir, eliminating overflow waste entirely
  • Managed project timeline using Gantt charts and progress tracking documentation

What Made It Different

  • Most designs only prevented overflow — ours captured and reused it, creating a circular water economy within the unit
  • Small perforated holes channelled excess water back into the reservoir, cutting consumption beyond what the brief required
  • Sustainability built into the architecture, not bolted on as an afterthought
